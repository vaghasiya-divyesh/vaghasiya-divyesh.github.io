Selected research projects

Ongoing

APN & PN functions, Sidon sets, and sum-free sets

With Dr. Lukas Kölsch. Investigating multiplicative subgroups of finite fields with Sidon and sum-free properties, their connections with APN functions in even characteristic and PN functions in odd characteristic, and the role of cyclotomic numbers.

Ongoing

“Good” rational functions for locally recoverable codes

With Anuvrat Jaindungarwal. Extending good-polynomial methods for locally recoverable codes (LRC) to rational functions. Manuscript draft completed.

2026 – present

Homogeneity degree of bent functions

Research collaboration with Lukas Kölsch and a team including undergraduate researchers. Investigation of a conjecture concerning the degree of homogeneous bent functions. Manuscript in final reading by the authors.

2024 – 2025

Autotopisms of finite semifields and rank-metric codes

With Lukas Kölsch. Investigated autotopism groups of finite semifields and associated maximum rank distance codes through explicit algebraic calculations.
